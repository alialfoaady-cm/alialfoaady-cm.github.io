Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clip Review Tool is a personal tool used by one person to create short video clips and publish them to that person's own social media accounts.

What data is used

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (username) so it can upload videos to that account. This data is stored only on the owner's own computer.

What we do not do

The tool does not collect data from any other users, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

The access token can be revoked at any time in the account's TikTok settings, and deleting the tool's local files removes all stored data.
