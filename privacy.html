Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Clip Review Tool is a personal tool used by one person to create short video clips and publish them to that person's own social media accounts (YouTube and TikTok).

YouTube API Services

Clip Review Tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is used

When the owner connects their own YouTube account, the tool receives an OAuth access token with permission to upload videos (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Google account data.

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (username) so it can upload videos to that account.

All tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload videos the owner has approved.

What we do not do

The tool does not collect data from any other users, does not use cookies or tracking, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account. Access can be revoked at any time: for Google/YouTube at Google security settings, and for TikTok in the account's TikTok settings. Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored data.
